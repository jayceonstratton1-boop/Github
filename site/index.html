Our story

From Pasco to two shops in the Tri‑Cities

We're Emilio Avila, a Marine Corps Reserve veteran, and Veronica Gonzalez, the husband-and-wife team behind Sip & Co. Our idea was simple: drinks that look as good as they taste and travel as well as you do.

When we opened on Sandifur Parkway in Pasco, the cans flew out so fast we ran out. Now we've opened a second home on Queensgate Drive in Richland. We're still local, still family-run, and not a franchise.
