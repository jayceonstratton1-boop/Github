Our Story

From a snack cart to Court Street

SnacExpress started as a snack cart serving Pasco one drink and one bag of chips at a time. When the lines kept growing, we opened our own storefront in Riverview Plaza.

Our pink shop has a "Snack Your Way" mural, comfy seats and plenty of spots for photos. You can stop in after school, after work, or just because.
