About the shop

A new era of cuts in the Tri-Cities

Established in 2024, The New Era Barber Studio sits at 411 W Clark St in Pasco. It's a professional, motivated and growing team of barbers working in a modern space lit up by its signature hexagon lights.

Walk in whenever you're ready, or skip the wait and lock in your time on Booksy.
