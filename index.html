Our story

Local, family-run, and always fresh

Sip & Co. is owned by husband-and-wife team Emilio Avila, a Marine Corps Reserve veteran, and Veronica Gonzalez. They wanted drinks that look as good as they taste and travel as well as you do, so every drink is made fresh and sealed in a can.

We started on Sandifur Parkway in Pasco. Now we've opened a second home on Queensgate Drive in Richland. We're a local business, not a franchise, and we're glad you're here.
