About the shop

A new era of cuts in the Tri-Cities

The New Era Barber Studio was founded in 2024 by owner Angel Almaraz. Angel opened the shop at 411 W Clark St in Pasco to give the Tri-Cities sharp, clean cuts in a professional, welcoming space.

Under Angel, New Era has grown into a motivated team of barbers, and it's still growing.

Walk in whenever you're ready, or book a time with Angel online.

Booking Policy

Please read before booking
No-Shows

Failure to show up for your scheduled appointment will result in a cancellation fee.

Late Cancellations

Last-minute cancellations will be subject to a cancellation fee.

Late Arrivals

Arriving 10 minutes late to your scheduled appointment will result in automatic cancellation, and you will be charged.

Booking Services

All appointments must be booked under the correct service you are requesting. Each service requires a specific amount of time, so booking the wrong service may result in your appointment being canceled or rescheduled.
