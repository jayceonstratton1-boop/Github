Our story

Made for slowing down

Sip & Co started with a simple idea: a great drink tastes even better when it's shared. What began as one neighborhood shop has grown into a second home in Richland — same recipes, same friendly faces, more room to gather.

Whether you're grabbing a quick cold brew on the way to work or settling in with friends, we're glad you're here.
