Our story

From one window in Pasco to two shops in the Tri-Cities

Sip & Co. is run by husband-and-wife team Emilio Avila, a Marine Corps Reserve veteran, and Veronica Gonzalez. They had a simple idea: drinks that look as good as they taste and travel as well as you do.

When we opened our order windows in Pasco, the cans flew out so fast we ran out. Now we've opened a second home on Queensgate Drive in Richland. We're still local, still family-run, and not a franchise.
